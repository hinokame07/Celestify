Celestify's Android adapter maps the upstream theme's documented/default visual intent to native Android rendering without bundling or executing its desktop JavaScript, SCSS, icons, analytics or remote assets.

The adapter source is `base/app/src/main/java/com/music/spotui/data/themes/NativeTheme.kt`.
